Thomas Passer Jensen

I am the Lead Sensor Fusion Engineer at Rokoko in Copenhagen, Denmark. I lead our team's research and development in body-tracking, finger-tracking, and sensor-fusion algorithms. My primary focus is establishing high-accuracy, real-time optimal state estimation frameworks for inertial and electromagnetic motion capture.

My research interests span Moving Horizon Estimation (MHE), nonlinear optimal control, structure-exploiting numerical solvers, and electromagnetic field navigation and mapping.

I completed my Master of Science in Electrical Engineering at the Technical University of Denmark (DTU), specializing in robotics and automation. During my studies, I was an exchange student at the Collaborative Robotics and Intelligent Systems Institute (CoRIS) at Oregon State University.

I completed my Bachelor of Science in Physics and Nanotechnology, also at DTU.

Selected Past Academic Research

Adaptive control for hindlimb locomotion in a simulated mouse through temporal cerebellar learning

T. P. Jensen, S. Tata, A. J. Ijspeert, S. Tolu
Proceedings of the 2020 Annual Neuro-Inspired Computational Elements (NICE) Conference, 2020
ACM DL  /  arXiv

Developed a bio-inspired neuromorphic control architecture combining spinal Central Pattern Generators (CPGs) and a cerebellum-like temporal learning model.
